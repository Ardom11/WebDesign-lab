About STRATA

STRATA — Autoculture without uncertainty.

We know what it’s like to submit an application to a car festival and wait for weeks in the dark, not understanding the selection criteria. Most car shows have become a “black box” with complicated forms and silence in response.

STRATA is changing the rules of the game. We have created a phygital ecosystem that makes the selection process completely transparent. Our calculator evaluates the characteristics of the car and shows the real probability of admission even before registration. No more wasted time.
