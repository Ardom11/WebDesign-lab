03 / Before you apply

A few useful answers.

Representative guidance. Final participation rules will be confirmed for each event.

Who can apply?

Independent owners and automotive clubs are invited to share their builds. Eligibility and vehicle categories will be confirmed on each event page.

What do I need to submit?

Your contact details, vehicle make, model and year, a short build story, recent photos and your preferred event. Include any requirements in the notes field.

When is the application deadline?

Deadlines are specific to each event and will be listed in its details. Dates and availability in this wireframe are placeholders.

How does the selection process work?

The curation team reviews the vehicle story, presentation and fit within the line-up. A review outcome and next steps are sent to the email you provide.

Does applying guarantee a display space?

No. Submitting an application is a request to participate. Acceptance, fees and event-specific conditions will be confirmed separately.
